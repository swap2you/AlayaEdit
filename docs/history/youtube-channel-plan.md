## What is actually hurting the channel

**The newest titles do not say who is speaking.** These public videos are the ones with almost no views:

- Krishna's divine protection.
- The battle begins!
- The spiritual leader is seen
- Who is the supreme Vasudeva?
- The true meaning of Vasudeva.

They read like automatic clip names. The videos from the same days that use a Hindi line plus “राधा गोविंद महाराज” are the ones getting watched. Only this small recent group should be retitled. The rest of the library stays.

**Chapters are missing.** Six descriptions in the whole library contain timestamps. The 83 lectures over 50 minutes are the videos people finish and the videos search can land inside. A chapter for a verse, a name, or a question is how someone searching “Kunti” or “Govardhan” lands on the minute Maharaj said it. The words in the chapter come from the lecture. They are not a new talk.

**The Community tab has been quiet since 1 July 2023.** That post was an Ambarish Maharaj katha announcement. Daily quote cards already exist as images for September through December (about 220 pictures) in `Daily Quotes - Swapnil`. One card a day is the post. The hashtag file next to those quotes includes `#1mview`, `#videoviral`, and `#trending`. Those tags stay out.

**The profile picture is a silhouette logo** (“Srimad Bhagavatamritam”), not Maharaj. The banner is a collage of photographs. The name and the handle are already searchable, and a name can only be changed twice in 14 days, so the name stays. The About line that says the Start Here playlist is “coming” is out of date. That playlist is already published.

**Another channel is ahead of these same lectures.** Content detection shows many matches, page “1–10 of many”, almost all from Srila Prabhupada Mission (about 39,900 subscribers). Several of their copies are 100% of the same katha, and those copies have more views than a lot of the originals. One Radhashtami copy shows 71,200 views. No removal was filed. That decision is yours. Until then, our title has to be the one a searcher recognizes as Maharaj’s own.

**Two Shorts are tests.** “test2” and “Test Short” have one view each. The Shorts that work are the real ones: Gaura Purnima and the Bhagavat Aarti are around 1,000 views. A new 61-second Short, “Radharani's Mahabhava”, has 175 views and people watched about half of it.

**The 613 Munch clips are wide, not Shorts.** They were exported at 1920×1080. YouTube only puts a video on the Shorts shelf when it is vertical or square. Adding `#shorts` does not change that. Posting all 613 in a week would also put a pile of near-duplicate clips on a channel that is already monetized. vidIQ’s September calendar already shows posts going out around 7:00 PM and 8:00 PM. The pace stays at two a day.

## 11,000 to 100,000

At the current pace the channel adds about 100 subscribers a month. 100,000 in two months means about 1,500 new subscribers a day. That is not what this library will do by uploading faster. The top video in six years has 28,000 views. A number like 100,000 subscribers needs a few videos, almost certainly Shorts, that travel far outside the people who already know Maharaj.

What can move the number, in this order:

1. Retitle only the generic recent clips, using the Hindi-plus-English pattern that already gets clicks.
2. One Community quote a day, from the quote cards, after a look at the face.
3. Chapters on the ten kathas people already watch: Radhashtami, Baal Leela, Govardhan, Bhishma, Gajendra, Vritrasura, Janmashtami, Kunti, Gaura, Ram katha.
4. One real Short a day, cut vertical from a line people repeat, with Maharaj’s face taken from a photograph and not redrawn.
5. Two videos a day on the Videos tab, not a dump of the Munch folder.

If a Short reaches a large audience, subscribers can jump. If none does, the channel still grows faster than 100 a month, and it will not be at 100,000 in May. I will not plan as if the second outcome is the first.
