# Locked look

Stylized 3D devotional film still. Soft morning light in Gokula. Fine faces, cloth with weight, dust and brass. Child-safe. No photograph look, no flat cartoon, no text, no watermark.

Krishna, for this age in the fruit pastime: a very small child. Skin the deep blue-black of a monsoon cloud. Large lotus eyes. Black curls. One peacock feather. A small white U on the forehead. Two arms. Short yellow silk with a narrow red border. A short garland of white, yellow, and violet flowers. Gold waist bells. Anklets. Barefoot. Mouth closed. No crown. No four arms. No flute in this episode.

The fruit seller: a dignified woman of the Braj forest. Warm deep brown skin. Hair in a bun. Modest maroon cotton sari with a narrow gold border. Small red bindi, small nose stud, thin gold chain, a few bangles. Cane basket. Kind, never comic.

Lane: white lime, one carved wooden door, tulasi on a stone pedestal, two brass pots, a kadamba tree, distant peacocks, morning dust. No crowd.

Motion, when a still is animated: subtle breathing, one blink, slight cloth movement, slow camera push. Preserve the face, the tilaka, the clothes, and the composition. No new limbs. No melting. No walking cycle unless a later shot is made for it.

The reference film people are copying is image-to-video, not a modeled 3D scene. On this computer the safe motion is the camera, because a 4 GB GPU cannot run a current image-to-video model while a lecture is being heard, and those models rewrite the face.
