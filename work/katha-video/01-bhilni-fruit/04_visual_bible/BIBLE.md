# Visual bible — fruit seller

Scripture for this episode: Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.11.11, as Maharaj speaks it in Vatsalya Lila part 6. Krishna here is the small child of Nanda and Yashoda, two-armed, in Gokula. He is not drawn as the four-armed Lord, and he is not given a king's crown.

## Rules

- Same face, same cloth, same basket weave, from shot to shot.
- No crowd. Two people is the maximum in one frame.
- Mouths stay closed. Maharaj is the only voice. There is no lip-sync.
- Hands are the failure point. Prefer a jar, a trail of grain, or a basket over a close view of fingers.
- Jewels are the Bhagavatam's `ratna`, which Maharaj calls gold and diamonds. Quiet light. Not a treasure hoard.
- While he says that he gives heaven and Vaikuntha, the picture remains the child. That contrast is the pastime.
- A small red bindi on the fruit seller is an ordinary village mark, held for review.
- No borrowed music, no second speaker, no cloned voice.

## Palette

Monsoon blue-black, marigold yellow, maroon, lime white, brass, dust gold, kadamba green. Morning, not night. No neon.

## Negative

Text, watermarks, extra limbs, extra fingers, melted faces, changed tilaka, modern objects, weapons, blood, fear, romance, four arms, a crowd, a copied character from another film.
