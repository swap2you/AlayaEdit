# What the opening is built from

Date: 29 Oct 2026. This is the design brief for the Vatsalya Lila books. The sample pages follow it. The full book waits for a review of those pages.

## The catch

Books that people keep, including the ones a reader means when they say “like a novel that travels,” do four plain things. This katha already does them. The book’s work is to keep them visible.

1. Start inside a day, on a question that costs someone something. Parikshit’s question is that opening: the real parents in the prison did not receive the happiness Nanda and Yashoda received. The sample begins there, then steps into the courtyard. Cosmology waits.
2. Let the wonder sit in ordinary work. Maids, a festival, a pot, a rope, a child who has just woken. The house is the world of the book. A reader stays because the kitchen is specific.
3. End before the knot. Each sitting of this series already stops in the middle of a motion. Those stops stay. The sample ends as Mother puts Krishna down and runs to the milk. The anger, the broken pot, and the rope are the next batch.
4. Give one line that can stand alone, after the scene, not instead of it. Here the line is Sridhara Swami’s: if the pastimes are sung while the work goes on, the Lord leaves His own rest and comes.

A cover works the same way. It shows one picture of the promise. This cover is the child, the yellow cloth, and the rope in a Braj courtyard. The title sits on the picture. It does not explain the rasa.

## The page

- One column, trade-paperback proportion, warm paper, wide margins, a hairline gold frame.
- Devanagari text in a book serif (Tiro Devanagari Hindi), with Nirmala UI on this computer if the webfont is blocked. Display lines in Gotu. Body size stays large. Leading stays open. A dense page is a lecture handout. This is a book.
- A shloka sits on its own plate, Sanskrit as the book has it, citation under it. The telling around it is Maharaj’s, polished into sentences.
- A painting every few pages, full width, with one line under it. The line does not retell the painting.
- Teaching follows the scene in a short aside, then the courtyard returns. Muchukunda is kept, and kept short, because Maharaj used him as a witness that the Lord comes for earlier love, and then the katha returns to the lap.

Sources for the page, not for the story: School Library Journal’s cover test (the cover is designed from a finished interior, and it has to carry genre, tone, and one promise); TypeEd on children’s type (large type and white space signal a younger reader; dense type signals an older one); Hadley Hendrix on children’s pages (a readable serif at a large size, the cover face kept off the body text).

## Three books, one katha

| Book | When | What a page is |
|---|---|---|
| Hindi reading book | Now. English only after this Hindi is approved. | Prose, shloka plates, paintings. The whole thirteen-part series as one continuous story. |
| Children’s storybook | After the Hindi reading book is approved. | Read-aloud for about ages 6 to 12. One feeling a page. Big type. Same paintings. Ages 13 to 15 stay with the reading book. One text cannot be honest for a five-year-old and a fifteen-year-old. |
| Comic | After the storybook. | Panels, gutters, caption boxes for time, balloons only for what the katha says the person did or cried. Wide shot, then the hands and the face. |

The reading book is prose with paintings. The comic is a separate panel book of the same morning. Both samples are in this folder so the look can be judged together. The comic and the storybook samples are the opening only. They are not permission to convert the rest before the Hindi reading book is approved.

## Pictures

Scripture-approved here means a dignified traditional scene: a small child, rain-cloud complexion, yellow cloth, peacock feather, courtyard, cows, tulasi, marigold, malati. Mother is fully clothed, maternal, absorbed in the churn. The plates do not illustrate the body’s detail in Bhagavatam 10.9.3, and they do not illustrate feeding. The verse stays on the text page. The picture stays the courtyard.

These three paintings are a first plate for review. They can be redrawn. They are not a claim of a historical portrait.

## Songs and shlokas

A shloka Maharaj quotes is given from the verse, with the Bhagavatam, Gita, or Chaitanya-charitamrita number in the plate. The purport is his telling, in book sentences.

When he quotes a line or two of a traditional song, the book adds the rest of that song only after the text is verified, and it names the poet. If the full text cannot be verified, the book keeps the lines he spoke. Nothing modern and copyrighted is pasted in to “complete” a song.

The first sample has the Padyavali story of the reflection as he told it. It does not add a Padyavali number he did not give.

## The missing sitting

Part 3 is not on Desire Tree and not in the lecture folder. The book does not pretend he spoke it.

When the continuous story reaches that gap — part 2 ends as Narada meets the women at the river; part 4 begins in the middle of his talk — the book inserts pages marked शास्त्र-सेतु. Those pages retell Srimad-Bhagavatam 10.10 from the point the recording stops to the point part 4 resumes, verse by verse, in the book’s own Hindi, with the verse numbers. They use a different frame so a reader can see they are scripture carrying the story across, not a recovered lecture. They are not filed as `part-03` of the lecture pipeline.

The children’s book, when it reaches Nalakuvara and Manigriva, keeps the pride and the mercy and leaves the adult detail of the river on the reading-book page.

## Registration

This copy is a private electronic review. It does not need an ISBN, and it does not need copyright registration.

An ISBN is for a book made available to the public. In India the Raja Rammohun Roy National Agency assigns it without a fee to a publisher or an individual author based in India: [isbn.gov.in](https://isbn.gov.in/). A text EPUB, a fixed-layout picture book, and a comic are different products. Each public product gets its own ISBN. The International ISBN Agency’s ebook guidance is the same: one ISBN where the format and the usage limits match, a new ISBN where they differ.

Copyright registration in India is separate from the ISBN and is optional. Public sale or a public upload waits until you decide that this arrangement of Maharaj’s katha should leave the house. The sample stays in this workspace.
