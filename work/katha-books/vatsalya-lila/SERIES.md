# वात्सल्य लीला — one book

The thirteen sittings are one story. The reading book does not restart at each file. Chapters below are the map. Only the opening of chapter 1 is written.

| Chapter | What happens | Lecture |
|---|---|---|
| 1. एक दिन | Parikshit’s question. Yashoda churns, sings, and Krishna comes to her lap. The sample ends as she runs to the boiling milk. | Part 1 |
| 2. दो अङ्गुल | The pot, the footprints, the stick, the rope that is short by two fingers, and the knot at the waist. Damodara. | Part 1 into part 2 |
| 3. भृत्य के वश में | The Lord shows that He is controlled by His devotee. | Part 2 |
| 4. दो अर्जुन | Bound, He sees the twin arjuna trees, formerly the sons of Kuvera. | Part 2 |
| 5. नदी पर नारद | Why Narada would curse them. The women leave the river. The two do not dress. | Part 2 ends |
| 6. शास्त्र-सेतु | Narada’s mercy across the gap, from Srimad-Bhagavatam 10.10, marked as scripture, until the sentence part 4 is already speaking. | No recording. Not a fake part 3. |
| 7. दरिद्र का तप | Narada on the austerity of the poor, already in progress when part 4 begins. | Part 4 |
| 8. वृक्षों की प्रार्थना | The curse’s fruit, the mortar between the trees, and their praise. | Parts 4 and 5 |
| 9. पूतना | The Surdas song, and the child who somehow escaped her. | Parts 5 to 7 |
| 10. बकासुर | Putana’s brother, in Kamsa’s company, and the swallow. | Parts 7 and 8 |
| 11. अतिथि | What a visitor should bring for the elders, the monkeys, the pigeons, and the peacocks. | Parts 8 and 9 |
| 12. अघासुर | The calves and the boys enter the mouth. He dies. A glance brings them back. | Parts 9 and 10 |
| 13. कथा की लालसा | Eagerness for Krishna-katha, told on the way to a railway station. | Parts 10 and 11 |
| 14. बछड़े और सखा | Brahma steals them. Krishna becomes the calves and the boys. Balarama is told. The fog and the dark night. | Parts 11 to 13 |
| 15. आरती | Brahma sees every boy as Krishna, offers the praise, and the book ends as the sitting ends, on the aarti of the Bhagavatam. | Part 13 |

Earlier pastimes — Putana, the cart, Trinavarta — enter when Maharaj brings them in, as memory or as witness, and they receive their own chapters where the later sittings tell them. They are not moved in front of this morning. The katha itself begins on this day.
