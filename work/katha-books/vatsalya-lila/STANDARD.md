# Vatsalya Lila books

Katha: HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. Series spoken from Srimad-Bhagavatam, Tenth Canto. Desire Tree files dated 3 July 2006. Lecture text: `work/vatsalya-lila/part-NN/Chapter.md`.

This folder is the book. It does not replace the lecture pipeline and it does not invent part 3 of the recording.

## Order

1. Hindi reading book, in batches of about twenty pages, until you approve the voice.
2. English of the approved Hindi.
3. Children’s storybook, ages about 6 to 12, read aloud.
4. Comic of the same katha.

Do not start the next form while the Hindi reading book of that stretch is still in review.

## Voice

Polish the spoken katha into book sentences. Keep his order, his examples, his jokes, and his refusals. He said he would not open, this year, the point that the Lord was also born from Yashoda. The book records that and does not open it.

Do not add a teaching he did not give. When he only indicates a pastime or a song, bring the cited verse, or the verified traditional song, and mark it. A शास्त्र-सेतु page is scripture across a gap in the recording. It is never written as his missing sitting.

Hindi stays standard Hindi. यशोदा, यमुना, वृन्दावन. Gaudiya names stay.

## A page

Follow `research/BESTSELLER.md` and `design/book.css`.

One scene, then a short aside, then the scene. A shloka on a plate with the verse number. A painting that is modest: child in yellow cloth, Mother fully clothed, no illustration of feeding or of the body’s detail in 10.9.3. The children’s book does not carry the adult detail of the Mandakini scene. The comic’s balloons only say what the katha already has the person do or cry.
