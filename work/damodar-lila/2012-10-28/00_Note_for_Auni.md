# Note for Auni — Damodar Lila, Part 6 only

Use this folder for **Part 6 only** (A06). Do not put these captions on Parts 1–5.

## What this is

- Series: Damodar Lila, Vrindavan 2012 (series A, DDM-2026-0013)
- Part: **A06 / Part 6 of 6**
- Speaker: HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj
- Place and day: ISKCON Vrindavan, 28 October 2012
- Spoken language: Hindi. Maharaj’s Bihari sound is kept in the meaning, and the spelling is standard Hindi. Yamuna is यमुना, Yashoda is यशोदा, tree is वृक्ष, not the ज and ब of the spoken sound.
- Length: 59 minutes 34 seconds
- Video: `DamodarLila2012_A_part06`

This replaces the old Part 6 auto-transcript. The katha is the close of the yamalarjuna pastime. Nalakuvara and Manigriva ask leave to return to Alakapuri, pray that every sense stay with Krishna, and the Lord, still tied to the mortar, smiles and sends them home with prema.

## How to use it

The captions are sentences, not a cut at every second. Put the Hindi SRT on the Hindi captions and the English SRT on the English captions. The times match this MP3 and the Part 6 master (59:34). Shift them only if a later edit adds an intro or cuts the start.

Where a verse is quoted, the text is the book’s text, with the citation in brackets. Hindi and English of those lines come from the same verse. Do not replace them with a fresh translation of the unclear chant.

Cited books: Srimad-Bhagavatam 10.10.37–43, 11.2.41, 7.7.55, 1.8.22; Bhagavad-gita 11.55 and 6.30; Sikshashtaka; Damodarashtakam.

The English is everyday English, in the mood of the katha. Hare Krishna at the end is the closing, not a broken name.
