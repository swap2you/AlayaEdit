# Clips from Vatsalya Lila, part 5

Times are from the start of this part. A row is one story that can stand alone.

| Kind | Start | End | Topic | What he says |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 3 minutes | 0:00:00 | 0:01:47 | Why say it again | A devotee asks why the same philosophy is repeated. The heart still says, this is mine. The Brahma-sutra says repetition is how a teaching enters the heart. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:01:50 | 0:03:25 | The hearers are we | Krishna is not the audience of this prayer. The world is His form, and He is also beyond it. The learned know the manifest and the unmanifest as His. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:03:25 | 0:06:39 | He knows the sneeze | Who thought what, in which second, and which body is coming next. A month of katha on one word of the first verse. A purse is stolen in the temple, and we do not know. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:06:41 | 0:10:30 | You alone are time | Past, present, and future. The demigods on the senses are not the controllers. Time is the name of His will. The rope and Narada's words were His. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:12:26 | 0:13:17 | God, and the hospital | Even in old age a man says, I am Brahman. A pamphlet calls the guru the Lord, and the Lord is admitted to a hospital. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:14:55 | 0:16:51 | The sun will not cool | A newspaper said the sun's heat is ending. Then the heat broke a record. At the end of creation it burns a hundredfold. His opulence does not decay. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:16:54 | 0:20:13 | Nature, and the person | He is mahat, subtle nature, and the three modes, and He is the person. A president is not a man who only looks. Where His eye goes, He also controls. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:23:24 | 0:25:30 | The book cannot see the eye | Knowing the length and color of a thing is not knowing Him. The eye sees the book. The book does not see the eye. Speech cannot catch the Lord. The soul can. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:27:51 | 0:30:24 | Brahma and the lotus stem | Even Brahma did not know his own cause. He went down the stem for the years of many kalpas, failed, and then heard two words: practice austerity. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:36:12 | 0:38:42 | The earth on the tusk | Varaha lifts the earth, laughing, and it looks like a spot on the moon. This child pulls a rope, and trees that thousands of elephants could not move come out by the root. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:40:15 | 0:41:41 | The mountain was an itch | Mandarachala, with its rough golden rocks, is rubbed on His back while the ocean flees. The Bhagavatam says it felt like a scratch, and He fell into a happy sleep. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:42:16 | 0:43:55 | The stones became boats | No crane, no pillar. Ten yojanas wide, a hundred long. The stones drifted until Rama's name was written, and then they floated steady. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:45:30 | 0:46:46 | What Dhruva was given | Devotees of Shiva, of Brahma, of Durga, received wealth. What Dhruva received, Ravana did not have a croreth part of. Dhruvaloka is not destroyed in the dissolution. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:47:49 | 0:51:02 | Welfare, and a good destination | He Himself is happiness, for the devotee who wants only love. Mangala is also food, a house, and the family staying with the Lord, and His feet at the end. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:51:02 | 0:53:22 | Quiet, because of the rope | Shanta means the Lord is free of anger. Here it also means the mischief has stopped. He is silent, and they are touching the feet of the bound child. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:00:23 | 1:00:56 | Why they leave Braj | They could have stayed. He wants this lila told among demigods, demons, and human beings. In heaven they will speak, and people will be joined to Krishna. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:01:38 | 1:03:56 | Only the name | Father is the king of kings. Nine treasures, Pushpaka, a body that Kamadeva would envy. They ask one thing: that Krishna's name not be forgotten. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:05:19 | 1:06:32 | Where the eyes should rest | They are being sent where the intoxication of wealth can return. The eyes that rested on women should rest on sadhus. Even a salon keeps the pictures hung. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:08:13 | 1:09:56 | Not a lisping child | Bound to the mortar, He smiles and speaks. He knew Narada's favor before the birth. Three years old, and no one had told Him. That is the Lord. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:09:57 | 1:11:50 | Like the sunrise | When a sadhu is not seen, the eyes are shut. When he is seen, bondage does not remain, as darkness does not remain in the eye that faces the sun. One day, only Narada. |
| Under 5 minutes | 1:13:54 | 1:16:54 | They bow to the rope | Love has been born. They circumambulate the bound child, bow to the mortar, the rope, and the children, walk north through Braj, and then take the sky. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:18:19 | 1:21:01 | The eyes fill again | Surdas: the young boy fills His eyes with tears, remembering the mother who loved Him and the hardness of today. A gopi says, Yashoda, look once at His face. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:22:23 | 1:24:18 | Butter and tears | He could not wipe the butter the monkeys had left, or the tears. The dark face is the sky, the butter a moon, the drops of tears the stars. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:26:56 | 1:28:02 | I told you so many times | Balarama weeps and scolds. Do not steal, do not do mischief. Today both hands are closed. Will you leave it now? He asks so that Mother will hear. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:29:00 | 1:31:08 | Bind me instead | The omen was bad, so he was not at home. Untie Shyama and tie me. She refuses. He almost shows who he is, then weeps and wipes Krishna's tears with his blue cloth. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:33:46 | 0:38:42 | How you know it is the Lord | An avatara is known by acts no body can do. Varaha, and this child uprooting the twin trees. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:57:45 | 1:06:55 | Every limb, and one boon | Speech, ears, hands, mind, head, and eyes. Sanatana on both ears. Then the boon: we have everything, give us the name, and keep our eyes on the sadhus. |
| Under 10 minutes | 1:08:13 | 1:16:54 | The bound child sends them home | He knew from the beginning. A sadhu's sight is the sunrise. Love is born. They bow to the rope and leave for Alakapuri. |
| Under 10 minutes | 1:25:14 | 1:31:26 | Balarama at the mortar | He comes running, weeps, scolds, offers his own arms for the rope, and then asks with compassion why Yashoda Maiya has bound the child. |
