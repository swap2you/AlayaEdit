# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 5

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 5. About 91 minutes. HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. It continues the prayer that part 4 had only begun.

Nalakūvara and Maṇigrīva are still speaking. The repetition is for us, because the heart will not accept that everything is Kṛṣṇa's. They say He is the world and He is beyond it, the knower and the controller, time, Viṣṇu, nature, and the person. Even Brahmā did not find Him by searching the lotus stem. He is known when the acts do not fit a body: Varāha, the bridge of stones, and this child pulling out the trees. They ask that every limb stay with Him, and, in the mood Maharaj gives from the Bhakti-rasayana, they ask only that His name not be forgotten. Kṛṣṇa, bound to the mortar, smiles and answers. He knew Nārada's favor from the beginning. A sādhu's sight is like the sunrise. Love has been born, so they may go. They bow to the rope, the mortar, and the children, walk north through Braj, and then take the sky.

Then the feeling of the binding. Mother Yaśodā has sent the gopīs away. Sūrdās: the boy fills His eyes with tears, butter and tears still on the face. Balarāma comes running, weeps, scolds, offers his own arms for the rope, and asks why Yaśodā Maiyā has bound the child. The sitting stops on that song.

Hindi in the captions uses standard spelling. The verses are from the book, with the citation in brackets. The boon about the nine treasures is told as Maharaj told it. It is not given a verse number.
