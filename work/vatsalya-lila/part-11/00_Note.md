# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 11

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 11. About 91 minutes. HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. It continues the sentence where part 10 stopped, at the railway station. It ends when Krishna understands that Brahma has stolen the calves and the boys. No notice has been sent out for the missing.

At a railway station there are pictures of women, and a bookstall selling only that story. Shukadeva remembered the comparison because it is true. The inborn nature of a devotee is speech, ear, and mind for Krishna-katha. At every word the longing grows. They never tire. In the world, women win by numbers. One woman, and lakhs of men greedy for her talk. Krishna's people are few. An acharya asks why so crude an example for Pariksit. Krishna is the supreme male, and the gopis are the higher nature, so the likeness holds. Worldly affairs are the lower nature. Vrindavan is fully spiritual. People want the forbidden pair to win against society. Krishna did the lila so that those who must read that kind of story would read his. Prabhupada writes the same of politics. Hear Krishna's politics at Hastinapura, and be purified. The lila has a place for every kind of person.

You are blessed. Hear carefully. This is a hidden thing. Gurus tell the deepest secret to an affectionate disciple. One person has many gurus. Dattatreya said one guru cannot tell the whole variety of the Lord. They must agree. One mantra, one path. If one says chant this and another says do not, the guru is finished. On a stage, speakers contradict one another. A tiger's cub among goats grazes grass until a tiger shows him his own form. Maharaj was sitting in a corner of an ISKCON program and called that talk a cheating. The nine yogis at Janaka's city answered Nimi as one. Do not sit in two boats. Then the answer begins.

He has saved the calves and the boys and brought them to the sandbank of the Yamuna. Soft sand, lotuses, bees, and birds. The food they carried came out of Aghasura just as it was. Earlier piety is not destroyed by a later fault. The day has climbed. They are hungry, and so is he. Water the calves here. They will graze nearby. He wants Brahma to be able to take them. They sit in rows on every side, and every boy sees Krishna in front. He shows opulence so each can eat the remnants according to taste. No pots from home. They taste the curd and rice, then feed him. A glance at a monkey, and the plate is filled. Sridama will not give, so Krishna puts his mouth to his. The acharyas wrote it. The sacrifice calls the Lord with mantras. Brahma stole the children in order to see the love, how Krishna would search or weep. He knew this is the Lord.

They water the calves and return. No one feels far. The meal is what cowherds carry from home. Eating together increases love. Logic has no standing. When Krishna lifts the morsel, all begin. Rows around him like petals around the saffron of a lotus. On him there is no front and no back. They sew large leaves. Some eat on an old leaf, a new shoot, fruit, birch bark, or a smooth stone. The remnants are left for the crows. A feast with no pot. A sannyasi's measure is one joining of the palms. A brahmachari's vessel is the same. The hand can be the pot.

Madhumangala jokes while eating. There is no propriety here. Praise the curd, and Krishna says give him all of it. Do you take me for a glutton. Thirty-two twice-born are serving you. The teeth are also called twice-born. Curd and rice spray from Krishna's mouth, and no one says stop.

Shukadeva sings the picture. The flute is tucked in the cloth at the belly, so a friend cannot snatch it. The horn is in one armpit, the stick in the other. The left hand holds a ball of curd and rice, more curd than rice. Pickle hangs from the fingers, lemon, chili, and karira, bitten and tucked back. He jokes so they will eat more. Laughter digests. Do not eat while weeping. Eat the prasada with joy, Nitai-Gaura-Hari, Hare Krishna. Heaven is watching. The Lord who stays silent when the sacrifice calls the calves with a shout, and bears the scolding of the gopis. A devotee understood. He is controlled by love, not by force.

The calves are conscious. They see the boys eating and fall to the grass. They go deep into the forest, out of sight. The boys' minds are on Krishna. Suddenly they drop the food. Where is my calf. Krishna says sit. He knows Brahma has taken them. He searches with the morsel, the pickle, and the stick. Then the boys who were eating are gone too. The lila energy covers his knowledge for a while. Then he understands. This is my son's doing. Vidhi's trick. No harm.

While they ate, the calves, made greedy for grass, entered the inner forest, far from the bank. Jiva says Brahma grew beautiful grass and led them out of the line of sight. It was the Lord's wish. Every mother in Braj, even the cows, wanted Krishna as her son. He wants to fulfill that, so the calves are removed. Not seeing them, the boys fear a ditch, the Yamuna, or a scolding at home for eating and not herding. Even fear is afraid of Krishna. Time fears him. Friends, do not stop eating. I will bring the calves. They are in this bush, so the boys will not all get up and come. Forgetting that he knows everything is also his opulence. Jiva wrote at length why the Lord becomes forgetful. He is really a child. He does not want the devotees disturbed. At the end of the chapter, the boys do not eat one morsel without him for a year.

An acharya's fancy. Go also means the senses. If the senses run while the devotees are with the Lord, he himself goes to bring them back. Do the worship. He will control the senses. He searches the mountain, the valley, the grove, and the cave, a child, with the morsel in his hand, saying I am eating as I go. In the nectar of devotion, he can leave everything in time, even his beloved. He does not leave the devotee who stays in the palm, in the heart. Devotee, and the rice. English sometimes shows it more clearly.

Shukadeva sings. Brahma, who had been astonished at Aghasura's liberation, takes the calves and then the boys and hides them. Shukadeva is sharp. Born of the lotus, born of the dull. Brahma is the greatest devotee and wants to see another charming glory, how Krishna searches and expands as the cowherds and the calves. One fancy: Brahma had prepared hell for Aghasura, and the Lord wiped the writing, so Brahma is a little angry at his eternal guru. Another: you protect the cows, I am your son, so protecting the calves is my work. Or he will ask the children who were inside what good thought was in the heart. Why does Krishna not throw him down, as he will scold Indra. An acharya: the Lord does not forbid the one to whom he gave the post of Vidhi. We ourselves will become the calves and the friends.

Krishna searches and does not find them. He comes back tired. The pots, the baskets, and the half-eaten food are gone. Brahma took everything. He searches both, again and again, ten times, on every side. He knows every second of the universe, and here he does not know where the calves are. Then the decision. All of this was done by Vidhi. Two meanings. The arranger did it. The open meaning is, my son did it. He knows suddenly. He does not ask anyone. Not a newspaper, not a television, not a pamphlet, not a radio call for a missing relative. The sitting stops there.

Hindi in the captions uses standard spelling. The Bhagavatam is given as Maharaj told it, without a verse number he did not lock. The commentator whose name was unclear is written as one acharya. Jiva, Prabhupada, and Dattatreya are named where the hearing is clear.
