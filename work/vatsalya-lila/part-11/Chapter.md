On every side. Every direction. Again. A second time, or three times, four times, five times, ten times. He is searching here. He is searching for the calves. The Lord is the knower of the universe. In the universe, where what is happening. What has happened. What will happen ahead. The Lord knows the matter of every second. But here he could not know where the calves are. Where they went. But at once, after this, he went ahead on this decision. That all was done by Vidhi. Vidhi has done all this. Of done by Vidhi, here there are two meanings. The Lord thought all. The arranger of all has done it. God has done it. But here the open meaning will be this, that Vidhi has done it. My son has done it. Krishna understood suddenly. Suddenly. He knew that Brahma stole them. The Lord did not go to ask anyone. It is not so that they put it in the newspaper and on television, where have the calves and the children gone, then it will be known. That also is not known. How many people are such that they put it out. They put out a pamphlet. They have it announced on the radio. And some relative of their house is lost. Missing. This sitting stops here.
