# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 1

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 1 of Vatsalya Lila, HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. About 91 minutes. The Desire Tree file is `RGS_Vatsalya_Lila_Part_01.mp3`. Year and place of the speaking are not on the recording.

He opens Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.9. Parikshit has asked what Nanda and Yashoda did to receive Krishna (10.8.46). Shukadeva answers with the day Yashoda churned the yogurt herself. The maids were preparing Indra's worship, the day after Diwali. She sings Krishna's childhood pastimes while churning (10.9.2, 10.9.3). Krishna wakes, stops the rod, and climbs into her lap (10.9.4, 10.9.5). She leaves Him for the boiling milk. He breaks the pot. She laughs, follows the footprints, and finds Him on the mortar feeding a monkey. She runs with a stick. Yogis cannot catch Him. She can (10.9.9). Kunti's verse is 1.8.31: the fear was real. She throws the stick away and takes up a rope. It falls short by two fingers. She does not know His power (atad-vīrya-kovidā). Shukadeva rejoices that a gopi has bound the unlimited Lord (10.9.13-14). Maharaj stops there. The rest of the binding is the next part.

Hindi in the captions uses standard spelling. य is not written as ज, and व is not written as ब. Verses are from the book, with the citation in brackets.
