# Clips from Vatsalya Lila, part 1

Times are from the start of this part. A row is one story that can stand alone.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 3 minutes | 0:08:03 | 0:10:09 | The complaints were love | Neighbors say Krishna ate the butter. Maharaj says the gopis wanted Him to come, to be caught, and to talk. Mother, in vatsalya, thinks her own butter is not good enough. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:15:33 | 0:17:33 | Krishna feeds His own reflection | From Padyavali, as quoted by Rupa Goswami. Krishna sees Himself in a pillar, takes the reflection for a guard, and tries to feed it butter. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:34:36 | 0:36:18 | Sing, and He comes | Sridhara Swami: even during housework, if the pastimes are sung with love, the Lord leaves His own work and comes. Yashoda was not calling Him. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:43:18 | 0:44:01 | What vatsalya includes | Feeding, bathing, dressing, kissing, and even scolding. Madhurya does not stay in this rasa. The Lord accepts being controlled. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:44:25 | 0:45:29 | Milk after a hundred years | At Kurukshetra Krishna was a hundred. Returning to Dvaraka, milk flowed from Devaki and from Vasudeva's other wives. First Canto. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:50:03 | 0:51:09 | Anger becomes blessed | His red lip quivers and the new teeth press it. Sridhara Swami: goodness ruling passion. The anger is touched by the Lord's lip. |
| Under 3 minutes | 0:53:08 | 0:54:12 | The blow on the empty side | He takes the small grinding stone and strikes the empty bottom of the pot, so the sound is greater. Then the yogurt pours, and He is worried. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:07:24 | 1:08:40 | Yama fears Him, He fears Mother | Crying is a child's strength. Time and Yamaraj fear Krishna. Brahma and Shiva praise Him. Yashoda scolds Him. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:20:04 | 1:21:23 | The rope of love | He can run from the minds of yogis. He cannot run from Mother. He used His full strength and was still caught. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:25:55 | 1:26:11 | Short by two fingers | The silk cord from her hair is tied to the mortar. As she goes to knot it at His waist, it is short by two fingers. The rest of this pastime is the next part. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:05:02 | 0:07:12 | Why the maids were sent away | Not the daily work. Preparations for Indra's worship, the day after Diwali, for as many days as Krishna held Govardhana. Then she churns herself. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:07:14 | 0:11:09 | Why Yashoda churns herself | She had never churned herself. Complaints made her think the house butter was not tasty. She sets padmagandha yogurt and churns after bathing, in silk. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:27:41 | 0:31:47 | From waking to the lap | He wakes, Mother is gone, He cries as if hungry, follows the sound, stops the rod, and climbs into the lap Himself. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:36:43 | 0:39:13 | Muchukunda and old devotion | Krishna comes to the sleeping Muchukunda because of earlier worship. Kalayavana burns. Our own practice is also singing: arati, japa, and katha. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:46:25 | 0:49:04 | She leaves Him for a pot of milk | He had left the ocean of milk. She leaves Him to save one pot. Sanatana Goswami: she put Him down so the child would not be hurt while she ran. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:54:31 | 0:58:31 | Footprints, and she laughs | The Lord who measured the world in two steps cannot cross the spilled yogurt. She returns, sees the broken pot, and laughs. No sorrow for the yogurt. |
| Under 5 minutes | 1:00:31 | 1:04:41 | A stick only to frighten | He is on the mortar feeding a monkey. She takes a stick, not to hit. She has never even shown a slap. She comes slowly. |
| Under 5 minutes | 1:15:50 | 1:19:28 | His own hand wipes the tears | Why she caught Him: the pot, the monkeys, the chase. The mother who used to wipe His tears is ready to punish, so His own hand is the only help. |
| Under 5 minutes | 1:26:28 | 1:30:49 | The rope cannot bind Him | Trinavarta, Shakatashura, the universal form. Atad-virya-kovida: love covers the memory of His power. Bhagavatam 10.9.13-14. Nothing is outside Him, so a rope cannot wrap Him. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:11:59 | 0:18:31 | She sings while she churns | Bhagavatam 10.9.2, Vishvanatha, and the Padyavali story of the reflection. The pastimes come by themselves. She has no other time to sing. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:18:51 | 0:27:41 | How to meditate on Yashoda | Bhagavatam 10.9.3. Silk, the golden cord, milk flowing, malati falling. A person cultivating vatsalya meditates on this, not on becoming Yashoda. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:31:47 | 0:40:07 | The meaning of Hari | He stops her because He trusts her love. Singing brings Him: the gopis, the rasa, Muchukunda. Katha itself is singing. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:49:30 | 0:58:55 | From the anger to her laugh | The blessed anger, the grinding stone, the footprints through the yogurt, and Mother laughing at His intelligence. |
| Under 10 minutes | 1:04:41 | 1:14:10 | The chase and the decision to bind | Acting afraid, yogis cannot catch Him, Kunti says the fear was real, and Yashoda decides to bind Him so He will not run into Mahavana. |
| Under 10 minutes | 1:21:23 | 1:30:49 | The stick falls, the rope begins | She throws the stick, lifts the rope, the cord is short by two fingers, and Shukadeva rejoices that the unlimited has been bound by a gopi. |
