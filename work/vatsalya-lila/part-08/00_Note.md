# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 8

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 8. About 91 minutes. HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. It continues where part 7 stopped, and it ends while he is saying what a visitor should bring for Vrindavan.

The boys see a still white crane, like a mountain peak fallen from the sky. They cannot run and they cannot stand. Shukadeva tells Parikshit the name: Bakasura. Putana is Baki. Aghasura, the younger brother, comes later as a python. Suddenly the beak takes Krishna. The boys fall as if lifeless. He is in the mouth, not yet the belly, and there he burns like red iron. The crane vomits. There is no wound on the soft body. Krishna catches both beaks and tears the body like a soft reed, in front of the children, with no effort. One reading is that the word mama, mother's brother, brings Kamsa, and the tearing is notice that the whole line will end.

The gods rain malati, which they know he loves, and they sound drums and conches and sing the lila at once. Life comes back into the boys. There is no scratch. They drive the calves home and tell it in every house. The cowherds look with thirsty eyes, as if he had died and returned. They will not say the child did the killing. Whoever came to kill, died, like a moth in the fire. Putana, the whirlwind, the cart, and the trees are told again because the news is fresh. Garga's words are never false: equal to Narayana in quality. A brahmana's blessing does not fail. Still, do not send the child alone.

Katha here is two people talking, night and day, only Krishna and Rama. They do not find the pain of the world. Prabhupada is read on the Bhagavatam and on the holy name. Then the sports of that age: hide and seek, and little bridges, with Jai Sri Rama, and jumping from branch to branch.

The next chapter opens on a forest meal. Krishna wakes before the children. Clay pots, fresh rice, pickle. Milk, yogurt, and ghee are enough. No tea. A horn, a stick, a flute, and the food on his back. The calves are countless. Prabhupada says hundreds of thousands. Raskhan asks to be one of Nanda's cows. Mix your own herd with his: serve the Lord's senses with your own. Each boy feels he is the one walking beside Krishna. No one asks why it is so early. Some nights they come at midnight, and Mother sends them to sleep. The gopis tell Uddhava the yogurt is kept and Yashoda will not bind him.

In the forest they take off the gold and put on fruit, leaves, feathers, and red clay. Surdas: in the evening the mother takes those ornaments off. Do not live on a father's greatness. Agastya drank the ocean, and he was a pot's son. They steal the baskets and give them back laughing. If Krishna goes a little way to see the forest, the race is I first, I first. He becomes as many as the boys. The demons at the pot of nectar said not you. Here the contest is centered on Krishna. Vrindavan is his house. There is no need to search. Then flutes, horns, bees, cuckoos, a bird's shadow, swans, the crane's meditation, peacocks, and the tail of a young monkey. The sitting stops on the plan for a visit: the elders, the monkeys, the pigeons, and the peacocks.

Hindi in the captions uses standard spelling. The verses are from the book, with the citation in brackets. Surdas is given as Maharaj told him, without a pad number he did not speak. Aghasura is named. He is not killed in this sitting.
