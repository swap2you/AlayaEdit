# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 4

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 4. Part 3 is not on Desire Tree, so this recording begins in the middle of Nārada's talk. About 91 minutes. HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj.

Nārada is explaining why poverty is an austerity. A poor man has the pain without the pride. Durvāsā and Mahārāja Ambarīṣa: half an hour, and a witch. Hunger thins the body and dries the senses. Saints sit with the poor. Śrī Caitanya would not meet King Pratāparudra. Then Nārada says he will take away the intoxication of Nalakūvara and Maṇigrīva and one day make them drunk on love of God. They become the twin arjuna trees in Braj, with memory of Kṛṣṇa, for a hundred celestial years. Nārada goes to Badarikāśrama to hear whether Nārāyaṇa approves. Kṛṣṇa, to keep Nārada's word, crawls with the mortar and uproots the trees. The cowherds think a demon has come and later leave Gokula for Vṛndāvana. Two shining beings bow to the child. Their prayer begins: Kṛṣṇa, Kṛṣṇa, great yogi. What they did, and what Nārada said, Kṛṣṇa caused. The manifest and unmanifest world is His energy. Maharaj stops while explaining why the Bhāgavatam repeats this.
