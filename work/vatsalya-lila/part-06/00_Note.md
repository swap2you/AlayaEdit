# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 6

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 6. About 91 minutes. HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. It opens on the Surdas song where part 5 stopped, and it ends while Upananda is still speaking.

Balarama asks Mother Yashoda how much yogurt the little brother drank, and why she bound him. He was not struck. Seeing Krishna weep, Balarama says she must have used the stick. If anyone but the mother had touched him with a finger, he could not have lived. Then both brothers weep, and Surdas asks that this image stay in the heart, birth after birth. Only the mother can untie the rope. Krishna signals Balarama to go.

The sound of the trees was held back until Nalakuvara and Manigriva had finished. The cowherds run, fearing thunder in a clear sky, and find the yamala-arjuna pulled out by the root. The mortar has left a track. The children say half a sentence: by him, the mortar went crosswise, he went between, and we saw two men. No one accepts it. A thousand elephants could not do it. Love, remembering Garga, will not hold the thought that the child is God. Nanda smiles, so the boy will not fear a second slap, and unties him.

Then the small lilas. He dances for a lump of butter. He brings one shoe at a time. The Lord of Lakshmi is under their hands. A Bhil woman selling fruit fills his empty palms, and her basket fills with jewels. From that day she brings fruit only for him.

Rohini calls from the Yamuna. The verse says bhagnarjunam, Krishna who had broken the arjuna trees. Maharaj also reads it as the name she called, and says Krishna remembered his future friend and did not come. Yashoda calls Krishna, Krishna. She calls Balarama the joy of the family and tells him to bring his younger brother. Food does not bring them. The birth star does: bathe, and give cows. She takes both hands. He is the crown of everything, and she knows only a son.

After the worship of Indra, the elders meet under a tree. Putana, the cart, the whirlwind, and the two trees had all aimed at Krishna. He is the life of Braj. Upananda, old in knowledge and in years, says they should leave Gokula. He says the calamities have come to destroy the children. The plural means Krishna. Love hides itself. Then, this child somehow escaped Putana, because of worship done in other lives. The sentence is still open when the sitting ends.

Hindi in the captions uses standard spelling. The verses are from the book, with the citation in brackets. The Surdas lines are from the Sursagar. Bhagnarjuna is given first as the book has it.
