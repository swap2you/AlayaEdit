So far that even breathing, or sending the inner breath out, was not possible for him. Closing completely, like rubber, they shrank in it, they stuck. They jammed completely. There was no hole anywhere by which anyone could breathe. He grew suddenly. He expanded himself with speed in the throat. What had been made a python became a real python. Today they jammed in his throat. Although I am unborn, and my body is imperishable, although I am the Lord of all beings. [Bhagavad-gita 4.6] The Lord himself says, I am unborn. The unborn has gone into the python. He has gone into the throat. One in whose throat a goat jams is called a python. This is the definition. That serpent who swallows one goat, one goat who passes through his throat, is called a python. A goat is called aja. But the real aja, the unborn, is Lord Krishna. A question comes. When he killed Bakasura, when he killed Putana, at that time he had not expanded the body. Why did he expand it this time. Could he not kill in another way. Srila Vishvanatha Chakravarti Thakur says, the Lord thought, at this time there is no one watching, so let me become large and kill. At another time people are watching. In front of the residents of Braj he cannot become large. In front of them everything has to be done while remaining a tiny child. Here no one was watching. So he grew at once. He manifested his opulence. In front of the residents of Braj he does not manifest his opulence. Perhaps this can be one cause. The snake began to thrash. From the head, the whole inside, every inch, had jammed in the throat. He had a very vast body, and still the whole throat was filled with Krishna. The path was blocked. The sight turned completely over. The eyes came out. He began to thrash here and there. He began to be bewildered. In the inner body the wind was blocked. The wind filled. It was not coming out. That same wind broke his head and came out. The life came out from the crown. It came out from the head. Breaking it like that. A very large part of the head of his body suddenly split and went separate. It is not that someone stays stuck in the body. It is not that some yogi will leave the body. Yogis leave the body by meditating. And one inside whose body the Lord has gone, how much meditation is happening for him. So the life came out from the crown. And by that all the life-breaths came out. First the wind came out, and with it all the consciousness went. No body was working. That is, he died.

जब वह मर गया, तब भगवान ने उसके पेट में प्रवेश किया। वहाँ सभी बच्चे, एक तो कृष्ण के विरह से, और दूसरे उसके पेट की ज्वाला से, सब मूर्च्छित हो गए थे। मर चुके थे। या जलकर मूर्च्छित होकर गिर गए थे। यहाँ परेत शब्द लिखा गया है। मर गए थे। विष की ज्वाला से, उस व्याल राक्षस से। बच्चे भी भीतर मर चुके थे। भगवान ने सबको जीवित किया। बच्चों को, और अपने साथियों को। फिर उनके साथ ही उसके मुख से निकले। वे मूर्ध्न से नहीं निकले। जैसे प्रवेश किए थे, वैसे निकले। और सभी बच्चों के देह में कहीं कोई दिशा का आश्रय नहीं था। कुछ नहीं था। अपनी दृष्टि से उठा लिया। हाथ लगाने की जरूरत नहीं पड़ी। अपनी करुणा की दृष्टि से सबको जीवित कर दिया। यह अर्थ है। भगवान कई बार ब्रजवासियों को अपनी दृष्टि से जीवित किए। कालिय नाग के विष की ज्वाला से भी गोप एक बार मर गए थे। उनको जीवित किया। यहाँ जीवित किया। भगवान की दृष्टि में सुधा का निवास है। चन्द्रमा भी दृष्टि से निकला है, जिसको सुधाकर कहा जाता है। जब भगवान की आँख से निकला हुआ चन्द्रमा सुधाकर है, तो आँख में कितनी सुधा होगी। सुधा से, अमृत से, जीवित कर दिए। मतलब स्नेह की दृष्टि से जीवित कर दिए। मुख से मुकुन्द बाहर निकले। इस पर एक आचार्य ने बहुत सुन्दर उत्प्रेक्षा की है। शुक्रदेव गोस्वामी मुकुन्द शब्द कह रहे हैं। मैं उसको पढ़कर यहाँ कहना चाहता हूँ। केवल मुकुन्द शब्द आया। भगवान के जितने भी नाम हैं, उनके कुछ निश्चित अर्थ हैं। वे सार्थक नाम हैं। अन्वर्थ। मेरे जितने नाम हैं, सब सार्थक हैं। पृथ्वी पर प्रसिद्ध हैं। पर यहाँ उन्होंने अपने स्वजनों को, बन्धुओं को, अघासुर से मुक्त किया, और अघासुर को ही संसार-बन्धन से मुक्त किया। यहाँ लगता है कि वे अपना मुकुन्द नाम बिलकुल प्रकट करना चाहते हैं। जो मुक्त करे, मुक्ति दे, उसको मुकुन्द कहते हैं। बच्चों को अघासुर से मुक्त किया। अघासुर को संसार-बन्धन से मुक्त किया। शुक्रदेव गोस्वामी यहाँ मुकुन्द नाम दे रहे हैं। भगवान के आशय को पकड़कर।

When he had died, then the Lord entered his belly. There all the children, first from separation from Krishna, and second from the flame of his belly, all had fainted. They had died. With the glance of his own compassion he brought all back to life. This is the meaning. Many times the Lord has brought the residents of Braj back to life with his glance. अब आत्मा निकला। पहले प्राण शरीर से निकल गए थे। प्राण में और आत्मा में भेद है। उस बहुत मोटे सर्प के देह से एक अद्भुत ज्योति निकली। महती ज्योति। इस संसार में वैसी ज्योति हम नहीं देखते, जैसी वह ज्योति निकली। अपने आलोक से दस दिशाओं को आलोकित कर दिया। और आकाश में प्रतीक्षा की। जब भगवान कृष्ण अघासुर के मुख से बाहर आ गए, तो वह ज्योति, देवताओं के देखते-देखते, उनमें प्रवेश कर गई। देवता सब देख रहे थे। अपलक नेत्रों से। उनकी पलक ऐसे भी नहीं गिरती। वे भगवान की लीला देख रहे थे। अघासुर को भगवत्प्राप्ति हुई। कृष्ण का अनुचर बना, नित्य लीला में। जैसे पूतना। यह बहुत अद्भुत बात है। भगवान ने गीता में आत्मा का वर्णन किया है। आत्मा तो सबका शरीर छोड़ता ही है। पर शरीर छोड़कर किधर गया, कुछ पता नहीं चलता। केवल उनका ही नहीं। बड़े-बड़े योगियों का, भक्तों का भी, आत्मा कहाँ गया, कुछ पता नहीं चलता। वैकुण्ठ में भले जाए, पर किस रूप में जा रहा है, दिखाई नहीं पड़ता। अनन्तर, भगवान की इच्छा से, वह ज्योति के रूप में जगमगाता हुआ निकला, और फिर भगवान में प्रवेश किया। सबके सामने दिखाया। एक आचार्य कहते हैं, आकाश का नाम है विष्णुपद। विष्णुपद में वह आत्मा थोड़ी देर क्यों टिका रहा। वे कहते हैं, अघासुर मानो कहता है, देखो, मेरे जैसा पापी भी भगवान के देह के सम्पर्क में आकर आज मुक्त हो गया है। और देखो, सत्य कहता हूँ। कोई भी व्यक्ति मुक्त हो सकता है। विष्णु के चरण को छूकर कह रहा हूँ। इस तरह मानो व्यक्त कर रहा है। विष्णुपद को छूकर कह रहा है। विष्णुपद में रहकर। और तब कृष्ण में प्रवेश किया। यह एक अद्भुत बात हुई। हमारे आचार्य लिखते हैं, ऐसा क्यों हुआ। क्यों नहीं चुपचाप वह आत्मा गोलोक चला गया। कहीं दिखाई क्यों दिया। इसका रहस्य यह है, भगवान दिखाना चाहते थे। अघासुर की ज्योति निकलकर भगवान में लीन हो गई।

Now the soul came out. First the life-breath had come out of the body. There is a difference between the life-breath and the soul. From the body of that very thick serpent a wonderful light came out. A great light. In this world we do not see a light like the light that came out. With its own radiance it lit the ten directions. And it waited in the sky. When Lord Krishna had come out of Aghasura's mouth, that light, while the gods were watching, entered him. The gods were all watching. With eyes that did not blink. Their eyelids do not fall in that way either. They were watching the Lord's lila. Aghasura attained the Lord. He became Krishna's attendant, in the eternal lila. Like Putana. This is a very wonderful thing. The Lord has described the soul in the Gita. The soul of everyone does leave the body. But having left the body, where it went, nothing is known. Not only theirs. Of the great yogis also, of the devotees also, where the soul went, nothing is known. Let it go to Vaikuntha, but in what form it is going is not seen. After that, by the Lord's wish, it came out shining in the form of light, and then it entered the Lord. He showed it in front of all. One acharya says, the name of the sky is the place of Vishnu's feet. Why did that soul stay a short while in the place of Vishnu's feet. He says, Aghasura is as if saying, look, even a sinner like me, having come in contact with the Lord's body, has been freed today. And look, I am telling the truth. Any person can be freed. I am saying it while touching Vishnu's feet. In this way he is as if declaring it. He is saying it while touching the place of Vishnu's feet. Staying in the place of Vishnu's feet. After this he spoke. Srila Shukadeva Goswami welcomes this feeling of Maharaj Pariksit. Well asked. Today you asked very well. O greatly fortunate one. O best of the devotees. In one verse there are two addresses for Maharaj Pariksit. O greatly fortunate one. As Maharaj Pariksit said about himself, that we are the most fortunate, fortunate above all, so Srila Shukadeva Goswami also says, in truth you are greatly fortunate. Toward Krishna-katha your taste is this deep. So you are the best of the devotees. Well asked. You asked me a very good question. It was asked so well that he entered samadhi. And now, before giving the answer of that question, he says, you have asked very well. One should learn to ask a question, in truth. Raising a finger, and saying I have one question, by doing that the thing is spoiled. The question should be about the Lord. When the speaker says, yes, you have asked very well. A fashion of asking questions has started. Some people think that a question must happen, whatever it is. Let anything be asked. But a question should be asked at the end of the katha. If a question is not asked, it felt as if the katha itself did not happen. Once I had spoken a katha. I had taken a class somewhere. Some one or two devotees had heard. Someone said, we can ask a question. I said, no. What question is forming. What will you ask in this. At the end, the question they wanted to ask had no touch with it. So in truth the question should be about the Lord. And to nourish the thing you are hearing. In support of it. Not so that what the speaker said for half an hour or forty-five minutes is cut down. It should not be so. What someone labored for an hour and spoke, the question shatters that same idea. O greatly fortunate one, from the beginning you have been the object of Sri Krishna's mercy. The Lord has had great affection toward you. Then, going into your mother's womb, he protected you. And at this time also, giving inspiration in my heart, he wants to free you from the bondage of the world. He wants to take you into his own abode. So at the time of birth also there was the same mercy of the Lord. And at this time of death you have no panic. Nor any agitation. Your mind is fixed on Krishna in this way. You are certainly greatly fortunate. And you are the best of the devotees. Among all the devotees of the Lord you are the best. Because of being a rasika of katha. Taking Krishna-katha with this much insistence. So you are the best among the bhagavatas. You are greatly fortunate for this, and you are the best of all among the bhagavatas for this, that you are making Krishna-katha new. New means fresh. Fresh even while hearing. The katha you have already heard, even the heard katha, you are making daily new.
