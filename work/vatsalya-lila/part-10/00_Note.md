# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 10

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 10. About 91 minutes. HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. It continues where part 9 stopped. The calves and the boys have entered the mouth. They have not been swallowed. It ends as he compares the eagerness for Krishna-katha with a man who is greedy to hear talk of women, and the sentence turns toward a railway station.

Aghasura waits for Bakari, the enemy of Baka. He will not close the mouth until Krishna enters, because Krishna would escape. Krishna, who gives fearlessness to the whole world, feels he cannot save his own friends. They seem like straw in the fire of the belly. What shall he do, so the wicked one dies and the friends live. He goes in himself. If Balarama had come, the children would have been stopped. Nothing is outside Krishna's sight.

The gods watch from behind the clouds, afraid of Kamsa, and they cry. Kamsa's company rejoices. Sanatana Goswami says they had the news. Krishna hears both. The python tries to crush them. Krishna expands in the throat. A python is a snake that can pass a goat. The real unborn one, Krishna, jams there. The Gita's line is given. No one of Braj was watching, so he could become large. The breath breaks the skull. The life leaves. Then he enters the belly. The children are dead from separation and from the fire. His glance brings them back, as it did after Kaliya, and they come out by the mouth. Mukunda: he freed his own, and he freed Aghasura.

A great light leaves the body, waits in the sky until Krishna comes out, and enters him while the gods watch. Like Putana. The Lord wanted this attaining to be seen. The gods offer flowers, dance, song, instruments, praise, and the shout of victory, each his own service. The sound reaches Brahma. He is astonished, and a little displeased that a sinner is sent to Vaikuntha. He will steal the calves and the boys. The dried body remains in Vrindavan as a cave. The children hide in it and call, I am here. They will tell the story a year later.

If one remembrance of the deity gives the Lord's destination, what wonder that the Lord himself was taken in. Chaitanya Mahaprabhu says the worship in the temple is he himself, accepting it. The year is left for the next day. Brahma-vimohana is coming.

Suta says Pariksit was given by Krishna in Uttara's womb, when Ashvatthama sent the weapon. The belly and the womb match. The pastimes seized the mind. How can an act of a year ago be called today. It can only be Hari's energy. They are eternally liberated associates. Pariksit has three days to live. Takshaka will bite him. He calls himself the most fortunate in the world. Jiva counts fortunate, more fortunate, and most fortunate. A low kshatriya, who once threw a snake on a meditating sage.

Prabhupada: a disciple may ask the guru any confidential thing. Shukadeva enters samadhi. He sees Krishna himself become the calves, the boys, the garland, the stick, the basket, and the food, for a year. The eyes stay open and do not see. Kirtan brings him back to the bank of the Ganga. He says the question was well asked. A question should nourish what was heard. You make the old katha new. Speaker and hearer stand on one ground. You are greedy for Krishna-katha as that man is greedy for talk of women. Vishvanatha says no other comparison was found. The sitting stops as the railway station is named.

Hindi in the captions uses standard spelling. The Gita line is from the book, with the citation in brackets. The Bhagavatam is given as Maharaj told it, without a verse number he did not lock. The commentator whose name was unclear is written as one acharya. Sanatana, Vishvanatha, Jiva, and Prabhupada are named where the hearing is clear.
