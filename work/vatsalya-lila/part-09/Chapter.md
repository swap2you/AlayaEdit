Saying this happiness of the children, the play with Krishna, Shukadeva Goswami says, in this way, he who is experienced as the joy of Brahman by the saints, as the supreme deity by those who have attained service, and as a human child by those under illusion, with him the heaps of accumulated piety played. [Srimad-Bhagavatam 10.12.11] This verse was very dear to Srila Prabhupada. Again and again, in his writings, he quoted it. Those children who in very many births had done a great deal of piety, had done good acts, had done the devotion of the Lord, they played in this way with Sri Krishna. Ittham means in this way. Meaning, in this way, in many ways, they were sporting with Krishna. Together they played. They were sporting with the Lord. They experience him as the joy of Brahman. They accept that the joy of Brahman is a good source, and they experience the joy. They are able to experience it only in meditation. But with Brahman they never receive a chance to play, to give the embrace of the neck. Like the sages beginning with Sanaka. They meditate on the Lord, and a chance to play with the Lord has never come to them. Great persons whom illusion can never bind. Who are above attachment to all the things made by illusion. In a thing produced by illusion they have not even a little attachment, and in Brahman, in the form of the Lord, whose mind has become stopped, they are called sat. Many times in the Bhagavatam. And asat is said of one who is absorbed in enjoying the things of the world, in taking pleasure. So satam means those who in their life have chosen the Lord. They have understood the Lord alone. And in front of the Lord, worldly things cannot give them any happiness. Even the happiness of heaven tastes flat to them. Enjoyment, liberation, nothing feels good. They stay overwhelmed in the form of the Lord. So they are called sat. Then the great saintly persons, the liberated persons, speak about Lord Krishna. They say this, that Sri Krishna is the Supreme Brahman, and by thinking of the Supreme Brahman they receive happiness. They accept, Krishna is the Supreme Brahman, Brahman condensed as eternity, knowledge, and bliss. But they are able to experience that happiness of the Lord only in meditation. They only contemplate. And by those who have attained service, as their supreme deity. Higher even than these saints are they who have attained service. Who have accepted Sri Krishna as their worshipful Lord, and who worship in the feeling of a servant. They serve the Lord. Who have a contact with the Lord, with his limbs. And in the eternal world, in Vaikuntha, or even in this world, who serve the Lord. Service. The meaning of dasya is service. And taking oneself as low, taking the Lord as supremely great, the Lord of all, the master of all, the Lord, they serve. They serve with great love. So such devotees are called those who have attained service. They have attained the position of a servant. But what do they take Krishna to be. The supreme deity. They take him as their own supreme god. They take him as the worshipful Lord. They serve in every way. But even those associates who live in Vaikuntha do not receive a chance to play with the Lord in this way. And the one whom the devotees of the servant's feeling take as the supreme deity, take as the worshipful Lord, take as the Lord, with that same Lord these children are playing.
