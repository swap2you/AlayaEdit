# What each part must contain

Speaker: HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. Hindi stays Hindi. English is everyday English, after the whole part has been heard.

1. Hear the audio. Keep a time on every word. Do not translate inside the speech model.
2. Read the whole part before writing. Correct Bihari य spoken as ज, and व spoken as ब, into standard Hindi spelling. यशोदा, यमुना, वृक्ष.
3. When a shloka, chaupai, Ramcharitmanas, or Chaitanya-charitamrita line is quoted, take the text from the book. Vedabase for the Bhagavatam, the Gita, and Chaitanya-charitamrita. Put the citation in brackets. If the verse cannot be found, translate what was heard.
4. English follows the sentence, not each sound. Keep Gaudiya words: vatsalya, Yashoda, prasadam, katha.
5. Files for the part, then upload to `09_Transcripts/Part NN` in the series folder:
   - Hindi.srt and English.srt, same times
   - Chapter.md — Hindi, then English, no times
   - Clips.md — table only
6. After the size check, delete the local MP3. Leave the text files and this progress note.
