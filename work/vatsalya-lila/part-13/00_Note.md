# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 13

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 13. About 74 minutes. HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. It continues the example where part 12 stopped, of fog against a night already full of darkness. It ends with the aarti of the Bhagavatam. This is the last existing part.

Fog cannot darken a night that is already dark. A firefly cannot dazzle the sun. Before Krishna, Brahma and Shiva are nothing. A small power used on a great person destroys itself. Brahma wanted to see the children weep. He himself fell into the circle. A saint tells the story. Become a cat, then a dog, then a cow, then a tiger, and then he wants to eat the guru. Become a mouse again. Do not try to overcome a great person. Brahma's intelligence is lost.

While he is still worrying, every calf and every boy appears as Krishna. A child says the body is blue, the cloth yellow, the flute in the hand, a peacock feather on the head, and a cow beside him. Crown, earrings, forest garland, Srivatsa, Kaustubha. A new tulasi garland, soft on every limb, offered by those who fulfilled much. In Vaikuntha the associates are at least three times this world. The worshipped form on earth is practice for that service. The smile is like moonlight. The reddish eye looks like creation, and the smile like maintenance, of the devotee's own desire. A week of the Bhagavatam has its formality, so he goes on.

From Brahma to a blade of grass, the perfections, the opulences, and the twenty-four elements are serving. Count them as twenty-four, twenty-five, or twenty-eight. Uddhava asked, and the Lord said it is a way of counting. Time stirs nature. Impression is not the same as nature. Even scholars of the Upanishads cannot touch the glory. One sight, and Brahma's eleven senses stop. He sits on the swan like a doll before the village goddess. Krishna draws the curtain of maya. The forms of Vishnu end. Brahma rises as if he had died, and opens eight eyes with difficulty. The form of Vishnu had made him sad. Vrindavan pleases him. Trees with fruit and flower. No natural enmity, no hunger, no anger, no theft, no craving for the senses. Only thirst for bhajan. What the eyes do not see is still that Vrindavan.

He sees the nondual Brahman alone, a morsel of curd and rice still in the hand, searching the grove, the cave, and the valley. The flute at the belly, the stick and the horn under the arms. Brahma jumps from the swan. A body like heated gold. Four heads, the godhood hidden. He rolls so dust touches the faces. The crowns move toward the soft feet and do not quite dare to touch. The all-knowing child looks astonished and does not speak, to the end. Eight eyes weep on the feet. He rises and falls for a long time, hoping to be forgiven. Then he wipes the eyes, bends the neck, joins the hands, and with a choked voice begins the praise. The first verse only.

I praise the body like a cloud, the cloth like lightning, the face shining with gunja and a peacock feather, the forest garland, the marks of the morsel, the stick, the horn, and the flute, the soft feet, born of the cowherd. Jiva writes that the dative stands where the accusative of naumi would be. This is not an obeisance to Vishnu in a yellow cloth. The earrings are gunja, not jewels. Uddhava says the ornament ornaments the limb. Here the gunja and the feather make the face more beautiful. The four marks are the morsel, the stick, the horn, and the flute. At the end, the son of Nanda. The singular is for Krishna. The friends are in the mind. He has praised Narayana before, Govinda the original person. Now he tells the summary of his life. May I serve this form. Soft feet. Even then he did not herd in shoes. He does not say Prabhu. Only sweetness. We are in Brahma's line. The one to be worshipped is Krishna who wanders Vrindavan, the son of the king of Braj.

He had meant to leave the lila and speak more principle. The devotees insisted. He had the commentary only through the eleventh chapter. When it had to be told, he remembered a library, and the commentary was there. The Lord in the heart made it appear. A person eager to hear is a greater devotee than the speaker, because speaking also gets praise. He thanks Krishna first, on Janmashtami, then the devotees who arranged it. They tolerated the misses. Next year, better. The devotees of Bombay asked him to come to Mira Road. He cannot promise from his own side.

It is an auspicious day. Even while the Lord was in the womb, auspiciousness covered the world. The Ramcharitmanas says the same. In Dvaraka the work of the gods is done. If he stays, minds will stay in devotion, so he leaves. The sages ask, whose shelter has dharma gone to. Devotion is the mark. The Bhagavatam remains, like a great magnet. Those who hear it and read it receive the good of living with Krishna. The lila was five thousand years ago. Janmashtami is kept by the purest method. Yesterday the temples were full, Radha Vallabha, Banke Bihari, Rangji. People have a natural faith. The duty is to give the pure message, the katha and the kirtan, so the love can grow. Darshan and an offering are not the whole of it. Then the aarti of the most pure Purana, the mine of dharma, devotion, and knowledge, fallen from the mouth of Shuka. Glory to the Mahapurana, for those who have faith. The sitting is complete. This is the last existing part.

Hindi in the captions uses standard spelling. The Bhagavatam is given as Maharaj told it, without a verse number he did not lock. Jiva and Uddhava are named where the hearing is clear. The Ramcharitmanas is named for the auspiciousness while the Lord was in the womb.
