# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 12

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 12. About 91 minutes. HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj. It continues the sentence where part 11 stopped, on the word for a missing person. It ends when one example has been given, of fog against a night already full of darkness.

If a relative is lost, they put out a notice, a picture, and a reward. Maharaj once disliked a dog that was kept. The dog left, and a notice offered a thousand dollars. He was glad, and he did not show it, because the people were in separation. Krishna knows without being told. All of this was done by Vidhi. When Brahma has done it, why forbid it. He quietly lets it be.

Then, to please the mothers and to please Brahma, Krishna makes himself both, the calves and the boys. The maker of the makers. Exactly as they were. Color, stick, flute, cloth, name, age, habit, the lunch basket, the way of meeting the mother. All of it is Vishnu. The Veda calls the world full of Vishnu, but that world is the Lord's energy. Here the Lord himself has appeared. Saying yavat once would have been enough, and still there is no difference in each one. A year later Brahma will see both sets the same. No laboratory, no birth, no quarrel with his son. The Lord himself grazes the grass of Braj, and enters as all of them.

Each calf goes to its own pen. In Bhojpuri, higrana, he separates them. No house is mistaken. The child who comes home playing the flute is the Supreme. The mothers rise at the sound. Even the dry breast flows. They nurse him as their son for a year. Brahma did well. Then oil, bath, food, ornaments, and protection, the folk tilak and the mantra. One Krishna is fondled in every house, so the verb is singular. He learns Maiya, Baba, and Bhaiya. Surdas: Mohan began to say Maiya, Maiya. Do not go far to play, someone's cow will strike you. Yashoda says the life is successful. He will not be mute. He has given this vatsalya to all the gopis of Braj. The husband of Lakshmi counts himself fortunate.

The cows come lowing and nurse, licking again and again, even those who have a newer calf. The motherly feeling was already there, in the gopis and in the cows, and in the Lord. Today's flood is new, because the son has been obtained. Love for their own son grows equal to love for Krishna. Near the end of the year Balarama asks why. Krishna laughs. I myself have become all of them. You would have been angry at Brahma, so I did not tell you.

One day, with five nights left, the cows are grazing on Govardhana. Krishna brings the calves near. The cows break into a run. Sticks cannot stop them. They forget the body. A soul fixed on the Lord does not stop. The cowherds come angry, and the moment they see the children they lift them and weep. Even the elders, who had discrimination, are filled. For a year this is the gift of himself. The throat stays lifted, waiting to meet them. At milking time the cows love these children more than the ones who have grown past the breast.

Balarama was not allowed to know. If he had known that Sridama and Madhumangala were stolen, he would have suffered, and as Shesha he might have struck Brahma. The lila would break. What Krishna wants, that happens. Then his own love grows the same way, and he asks whose power this is. Not a god's, not a demon's. Krishna's own wish. With the eye of knowledge, every child, cloth, and ornament appears as Krishna. Why so many, he has not yet understood.

He asks. These are not the lords of the gods, nor sages. You alone are shining, even in what looks different, like Subala, Madhumangala, or a calf. Say it in brief. Are you playing a drama. Krishna tells him the whole thing. Five nights remain. Balarama is pleased. Brahma did well, or you inspired him. Why did you not tell at the start. One acharya says, this is how divine knowledge is obtained. Do not be proud of being the elder. Brahma, later, will not ask, and will fall into circles. Balarama knew. The affection was not confusion. There is no darkness inside the sun.

From time without beginning the soul searches for a beloved and for bliss. Love of a body ends when the soul leaves. Krishna is dense bliss. By another road, every soul is searching for him. Balarama's conduct is the evidence.

Meanwhile Brahma's own time is a truti, the pierce of a needle through a lotus leaf, while a year passes in Braj. He comes to see a further charming glory. The same calves and boys are playing, and the ones he stole are still asleep on the bed of his maya. He counts. Both sides match. He cannot tell which is real. The heart pounds. He had come to see the glory, and he has offended. He who bewilders the universe, himself free of bewilderment, is bewildered by his own maya. He wanted to see Krishna weep and search. Krishna became them, and did not weep. If Brahma had not come to look, the Lord would have remained so without end. Shukadeva gives the example. Darkness born of fog cannot darken a night that is already full of darkness, and cannot remove it either. The moon would show the fog. Otherwise it is a dense night. One example has been given. The sitting stops there.

Hindi in the captions uses standard spelling. The Bhagavatam is given as Maharaj told it, without a verse number he did not lock. Surdas is named where the song is clear. The commentator whose name was unclear is written as one acharya.
