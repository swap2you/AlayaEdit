# Clips from Vatsalya Lila, part 12

Times are from the start of this part. A row is one story that can stand alone.

| Under 5 minutes | 0:00:00 | 0:01:59 | The notice for the dog | A lost relative gets a picture and a reward. Maharaj was glad the dog was gone, and did not show it. Krishna knows without being told. Vidhi did it, so he lets it be. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:02:00 | 0:08:20 | He becomes both | To please the mothers and Brahma, Krishna becomes every calf and every boy. Color, stick, flute, age, and habit. Nothing there is other than Vishnu. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:08:20 | 0:15:25 | No one else can do this | A year later both sets are the same. No laboratory. He does not quarrel with his son. The Lord himself grazes the grass of Braj, and enters as all of them. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:15:25 | 0:22:38 | The milk of love | Each calf to its own pen. The flute brings the mothers out. Even the dry breast flows. They nurse the Supreme as their son for a year. Brahma did well. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:22:38 | 0:32:29 | Maiya, Baba, Bhaiya | Oil, bath, food, and the black tilak of protection. One Krishna is fondled in every house. Surdas: Mohan says Maiya. Yashoda says the life is successful. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:32:32 | 0:38:57 | The cows answer the lowing | The cows nurse and lick, even those with a newer calf. The motherly feeling was already there. Today's flood is new, because the son has been obtained. |
| Under 5 minutes | 0:39:08 | 0:43:12 | I have become all of them | Love for their own son grows equal to love for Krishna. Near the end of the year Balarama asks. Krishna laughs. You would have stopped it. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:43:13 | 0:49:04 | The cows break from Govardhana | Five nights remain. The cows see the calves and run. Sticks cannot stop them. They forget the body. A soul fixed on the Lord does not stop. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:49:04 | 0:55:45 | The gift of himself | The angry cowherds lift the children and weep. Even the elders are filled. For a year he gives himself. |
| Under 10 minutes | 0:56:39 | 1:02:27 | Why Balarama was not told | The longing grows past the love for the older children. If Balarama had known, he might have struck Brahma. Shesha would have broken the lila. |
| Under 10 minutes | 1:02:28 | 1:07:32 | Whose power is this | Balarama's own love is growing the same way. It is not a demon's magic, and not another's. It is Krishna's own wish. |
| Under 3 minutes | 1:07:35 | 1:10:03 | The eye of knowledge | With a little attention every child, cloth, and ornament appears as Krishna. Why so many Krishnas, he has not yet understood. |
| Under 10 minutes | 1:10:03 | 1:16:17 | Say it in brief | These are not gods or sages. You alone are shining. Krishna tells him, with five nights left. One acharya: the great ones ask. There is no darkness in the sun. |
| Under 5 minutes | 1:16:19 | 1:19:34 | The search for the beloved | Every soul wants a beloved and wants bliss. Love of a body ends when the soul leaves. Krishna is dense bliss. Balarama's conduct is the evidence. |
| Under 10 minutes | 1:19:35 | 1:26:20 | A truti, and a year | A year in Braj is one pierce of a lotus leaf for Brahma. Both sets match. He cannot tell which is real. The heart pounds. He had come to see a charming glory. |
| Under 5 minutes | 1:26:20 | 1:30:38 | Fog on a dark night | Bewildered by his own maya, he cannot bewilder the Lord. If he had not come to look, Krishna would have remained the children forever. The sitting stops on the first example. |
