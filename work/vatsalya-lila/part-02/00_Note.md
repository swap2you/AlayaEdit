# Note for Auni — Vatsalya Lila, part 2

English first, then Hindi.

This is part 2. It continues part 1. About 90 minutes. HH Radha Govind Das Goswami Maharaj.

He finishes Śrīmad-Bhāgavatam 10.9.13-14: the rope is Krishna's own energy, so it cannot bind Him. Then the ropes of Gokula, every one short by two fingers (10.9.15-17). Mother is soaked with sweat. The Lord's energies refuse the binding. He sees her labor and accepts the knot (10.9.18). One finger is her effort, the other is His mercy. That is why He is Dāmodara. The Lord who controls Brahmā, Śiva, and the universe is controlled by His servant (10.9.19). Brahmā, Śiva, and Lakṣmī did not receive what Yaśodā received (10.9.20). The son of the gopī is obtained by devotion in the mood of Braj (10.9.21). He is Devakī's son, and He is Yaśodā's son.

Mother returns to the housework. Bound to the mortar, Krishna sees the twin arjuna trees, Nalakūvara and Maṇigrīva (10.9.22-23). Parīkṣit, himself cursed to die in seven days, asks why Nārada cursed them (10.10.1). They were Kuvera's sons and Śiva's servants, and still they drank vāruṇī in the garden of Kailāsa, on the bank of the Gaṅgā, with women (10.10.2-4). Nārada arrives by chance, which was their fortune (10.10.5). The women dress in fear. The brothers will not. Maharaj stops there. The curse is the next part.
