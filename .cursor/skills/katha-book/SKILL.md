# Katha book

Read `work/katha-books/vatsalya-lila/STANDARD.md` and `research/BESTSELLER.md` before writing pages. The lecture text is `work/vatsalya-lila/part-NN/Chapter.md`. The map of the whole book is `SERIES.md`.

## Order

Hindi reading book first, in batches of about twenty pages. English only after that Hindi is approved. Then the children's storybook (read-aloud, about ages 6 to 12). Then the comic. A style sample of a later form may sit beside the Hindi sample. Do not convert the rest of the series into that form until the Hindi of that stretch is approved.

## Voice

Polish Maharaj's spoken katha into book sentences. Keep his order, examples, and refusals. Do not add a teaching he did not give.

A quoted shloka comes from the verse, with the citation on the plate. A song is completed only when the traditional text is verified and the poet is named. Otherwise keep his lines.

Part 3 of this series has no recording. Bridge it with pages marked शास्त्र-सेतु, from Srimad-Bhagavatam, in a different frame. Never write those pages as his missing lecture, and never file them as lecture `part-03`.

## Pages

Use `design/book.css`. One column, warm paper, Devanagari serif, shloka plates, a painting every few pages. Paintings stay modest: a clothed child, Mother fully clothed, no feeding scene, no illustration of the body's detail in Bhagavatam 10.9.3. Comic balloons only speak what the katha already has the person do or cry. The children's book leaves the adult Mandakini detail on the reading-book page.

Open `hindi/ebook.html` for the voice of the first batch. Continue from where that batch stops.

Do not upload the book to the lecture Drive folder unless asked. Do not create a second Vatsalya series.
